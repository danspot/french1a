      Say goodbye

        Build a mini-conversation

        1. Greet your teacher
        2. Say how you are
        3. Say “See you tomorrow”
        Accent marks matter in writing: zéro, ça, très, à, bientôt. In the online number quiz, answers without accents are accepted while you learn.

        Choose the best farewell

        1 · School ends for today.You will see your friend tomorrow.
        2 · Class is beginning.Greet your teacher politely.
        3 · Lunch is in two hours.You will see your friend then.
        4 · You’ll meet again tonight.Say “See you later.”
        5 · You’re leaving now.You do not know exactly when you’ll meet again.
        6 · A dramatic final goodbye!Which rare farewell fits?
