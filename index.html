Part 1 · Les mois de l’année

Twelve months, five ways to practice

January is month 1; December is month 12. Tap a speaker, listen, and repeat the French name aloud.

French month names use lowercase letters. Look closely at février, août, and décembre. Practice accepts missing accents and shows the correct spelling after every answer.

Part 2 · Les mots manquants

Complete the conversation

Type the missing word or words in a familiar sentence. Each blank has its own Hear missing word hint. After checking, hear the whole sentence and say it aloud.

Review the Chapter 1 phrases and personal questions from class, plus a few common polite expressions. Use tu with a friend and vous for a formal greeting or more than one person.
